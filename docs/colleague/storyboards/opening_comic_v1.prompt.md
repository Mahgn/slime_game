## Prompt

> Use case: illustration-story.
> Asset type: exploratory opening storyboard comic page for a humorous 3D third-person adventure game, Slizi.
> Input images: Image 1 is the exact visual reference for the hero from the front; Image 2 is the exact visual reference from the side. Keep the hero recognizable in EVERY panel: one small translucent aqua-green rounded cube of slime, soft squishy edges, two dark green oval eyes with two tiny white highlights each; no arms, legs, mouth, clothing, exposed core, back droplets, or human features.
> Primary request: a single PORTRAIT comic page, exactly SIX large sequential rectangular panels in a clean 2-column by 3-row grid, read left-to-right then top-to-bottom, with consistent character and location, thick white gutters and loose dark ink panel borders. These are quick cinematic storyboard sketches, not finished game art.
> Panel 1 (top left): on a rainy, mossy surface at dusk, a small herd of similar slimes crosses a wet stone toward a distant big puddle; Slizi is last and looks back nervously. Keep the hero visibly distinct, slightly brighter aqua.
> Panel 2 (top right): the stone gives way beneath Slizi; one small member of the herd looks back in surprise as Slizi tips into a narrow dark hole, squash-and-stretch slapstick.
> Panel 3 (middle left): dynamic long vertical view down a natural rocky shaft; Slizi tumbles past roots and a mushroom, eyes wide, with a strong downward motion arc; no violence.
> Panel 4 (middle right): Slizi lands with a harmless spectacular SPLAT in a shallow underground pool, flattened into a jelly puddle for a beat; one tiny bubble floats up; gentle comedy.
> Panel 5 (bottom left): Slizi reforms into the familiar rounded cube, seen from behind/three-quarter, gazing up at a tiny unreachable opening high overhead with two tiny herd silhouettes and cool daylight. Quiet emotional beat.
> Panel 6 (bottom right): wide over-the-shoulder view of Slizi looking across the lower cavern: a readable winding route of broad stone ledges climbs toward warm light above; a little movement-ready posture says “I’m going up.” The same shaft and pool are visible behind him so geography is clear.
> Style/medium: expressive pencil-and-ink comic storyboard with restrained watercolor washes, cinematic framing, playful adventure tone, soft atmospheric depth. Character contour readable against backgrounds. Palette cool teal, moss and stone with warm golden light at the ascent.
> Constraints: EXACTLY six panels, sequential, clear silhouettes, no dialogue balloons, no words, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no watermarks. Keep any other slimes only at the surface or tiny silhouettes at the hole; Slizi is alone underground.
